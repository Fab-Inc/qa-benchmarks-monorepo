Linguistic Knowledge Benchmark — Native Speaker Review

About this dataset

This dataset is part of the Linguistic Knowledge Benchmark, a project that measures how well AI language models understand the formal linguistics of a specific language — its spelling rules, grammar, morphology, vocabulary, and discourse conventions. Questions are written in English and ask about the target language (e.g. Swahili or Luganda). The goal is not to test whether a model can speak the language, but whether it knows how the language works.

Question format

Each question belongs to a domain (e.g. Phonology, Grammar, Vocabulary) and a school grade level. There are two formats:

  • Multiple choice — four options A–D, one correct answer (shown highlighted in green).
  • Short form — an open answer expected in 1–3 words.

What we expect from you

As a native speaker, your role is to verify that each question is linguistically correct, unambiguous, and appropriate. Specifically:

  • Is the expected answer actually correct in the language?
  • For MCQ: are the wrong options clearly wrong (no misleading distractors)?
  • Is the question wording clear and unambiguous?
  • Does the question feel natural and culturally appropriate?

The three annotation statuses

✅
Keep
Question and answer are correct and suitable. No changes needed.
✏️
Revise
There is an error or issue, but the question is worth keeping with corrections.
❌
Drop
The question is too flawed, irrelevant, or misleading to salvage.

💡 How the tool works

  • At the top of each question, first check its Grade / Domain / SVR / GEEAP tags — click ⓘ on any tag to see what its values mean, and use the dropdown to fix it if it's wrong. You must confirm the tags before you can choose a status.
  • Click Keep to accept a question — the tool moves to the next one automatically. Keep means the question, answer, and every tag are correct.
  • Click Revise or Drop to flag a question. You must add a note before moving on.
  • You can go back to any previous question using the ← Previous button.
  • Your progress is auto-saved in your browser — you can close and reopen without losing work.
  • You can stop at any point: click Finish & Export to save your progress as a CSV. Loading that same file later resumes exactly where you left off (or choose to start it fresh when prompted).
